# The World in One Image — Sunday, 22 November 2026

One front-page collage that sums up 22 November 2026 in world news. Six
stories, six panels:

1. **Mexico — "El Chapo Isidro" captured.** Mexican special forces arrested
   cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa; 11 people were killed
   in the violence around the operation.
2. **Middle East — Iran strikes Bahrain.** An Iranian strike hit a civilian
   industrial facility in Bahrain as air defences intercepted further attacks.
3. **Climate — COP31 talks in overtime.** The UN climate summit in Antalya,
   Turkey entered a second day of overtime, deadlocked over fossil fuels.
4. **Summit — Trump, Xi and Putin meet.** The leaders of the US, China and
   Russia met in Shenzhen and agreed to begin talks on nuclear risk reduction.
5. **Markets — US stocks fall in AI sell-off.** US stocks fell sharply as a
   sell-off in AI and chip shares pushed the Dow and S&P 500 into correction.
6. **Baltic Sea — Russian ship hit by drone.** A Russian cargo ship was hit
   by a drone in the Baltic Sea; the crew was evacuated as the fire was fought.

The image is `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG). `main.ts` copies it to the output
directory as `entry.png`, using only the Node.js standard library.

## Run it

```bash
node main.ts
# or, with a custom output directory:
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```

This writes `entry.png` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`),
creating the directory if needed, and verifies the copy is byte-identical
to the source.
